TERX001: free video downloader for Android

Save videos from TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, X, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit and Threads. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. About 24 MB.

Download the APKView source code

What it does

  • Paste a link, or share it straight to TERX001 from another app. Pasting TikTok's whole share message works too.
  • Shows which site the link is from, then a progress bar.
  • Open or share the finished video, and see everything you downloaded in the in-app history.
  • Updates its download engine (yt-dlp) automatically, so it keeps working when sites change.
  • Runs entirely on your phone. No server, no account, nothing sent to us.
  • Free and open source under the GNU GPL v3.

How to install

  1. Open the Releases page and download TERX001-v0.4.3.apk (or the newest version).
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow installing apps from this source.
  3. Open TERX001, paste a video link and tap Download. Videos are saved in Download/TERX001.

Needs Android 10 or newer on a 64-bit (arm64) phone. If you installed an older test build, uninstall it first.

Questions

Is TERX001 free?
Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, no accounts.
Why isn't it on Google Play?
Stores restrict downloader apps for these sites, so TERX001 is distributed as a signed APK from GitHub. You can read every line of the source code.
Is there an iPhone version?
No, TERX001 is Android only.
Is it legal to use?
Download only content you own or have permission to save, and respect each site's terms. Read the terms and copyright guidance. TERX001 is not affiliated with TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, Facebook, X, YouTube, Pinterest, Reddit or Threads.
What does it collect?
Nothing. See the privacy policy.
Something doesn't work. What now?
Tap Update yt-dlp in the app, then open an issue with the link type and the message you see.
