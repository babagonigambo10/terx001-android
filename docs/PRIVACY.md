# TERX001 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. Publisher: SIDI TECH.

TERX001 is a video downloader that runs on your phone. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics
and no tracking.

## What the app stores
- The videos you download, in `Download/TERX001` on your device. You can open, share or delete them
  with any file manager.
- The time of the last yt-dlp update, saved privately on your device so the app updates at most once a day.

## What leaves your device
- When you tap Download, the app connects to the website of the link you pasted (for example TikTok
  or Instagram) to fetch the video. That website sees your IP address and handles the request under
  its own privacy policy.
- The app updates its download engine (yt-dlp) from the engine project's release servers on GitHub, at
  most once a day. GitHub sees your IP address when this happens.
- We run no server for this app. SIDI TECH receives none of your links, videos or personal data.

## Permissions
- Internet: needed to download.
- No storage, contacts, camera, microphone or location permission is requested.

## Children
TERX001 is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the app's source code.
